# Science By Hugs — Blue Reactor Brand System

**Status:** APPROVED / SOURCE OF TRUTH  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Approved:** 2026-09-28

## Brand architecture
Science By Hugs is the master brand. NEXUS, CORE, and PULSE are product sub-brands and must share one visual system while retaining a focused product accent.

### Official lockups and taglines
- **SCIENCE BY HUGS** — *Built for the research ahead.*
- **NEXUS · SCIENCE BY HUGS** — *Explore. Connect. Order.*
- **CORE · SCIENCE BY HUGS** — *Control. Operate. Manage.*
- **PULSE · SCIENCE BY HUGS** — *Track. Measure. Evolve.*

Do not introduce alternate taglines on product surfaces.

## Shared Blue Reactor palette

- Surface 2 — `#121923`

### Product extensions
- **NEXUS:** Cobalt + Cyan. Orbital/reactor language.
- **CORE:** Graphite + Cobalt + Metallic Silver. Precise command-center geometry.
- **PULSE:** Cyan + Teal `#21E6C1` + Violet `#8B6CFF`. Biometric/waveform language. Danger red is semantic only.

## Typography
- Display/headings: **Space Grotesk**
- Body/UI/data: **Inter**
- System labels: uppercase with restrained tracking

## Logo rules
- Use only the canonical product lockup for the named product.
- Product lockups always include “SCIENCE BY HUGS”.
- Use the product app icon for that product's PWA/icon context.
- Use the SBH monogram only for master-brand contexts.
- Never recolor logos outside the approved shared/product palettes.
- Do not create alternate product marks for individual pages.

## UI rules
- Jet Black is the primary background.
- Panels use Surface 1 / Surface 2 with thin cyan/blue structure.
- Primary actions use cobalt-to-cyan energy.
- Glow is an accent, not a fill.
- Status colors remain semantic and never replace product branding.
- Responsive/mobile variants may simplify layout, not identity.
